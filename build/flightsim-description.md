# FlightBrief — descriptions pour flightsim.to

## English

✈️ FlightBrief — your whole flight, from briefing to butter landing, in one free app

Stop juggling five tools. FlightBrief prepares your flight, tracks it live, grades your landing, keeps your logbook and careers, and turns your stream into a show your chat plays along with. Free, no account, everything stays on your PC.

🧭 PLAN
- One-click SimBrief import: OFP, route, top of climb and top of descent
- Live map with online VATSIM and IVAO controllers, and your friends flying right now
- "Where should I fly today?": three destination ideas based on your time, aircraft, real weather and online ATC

🛬 FLY
- Live tracking through SimConnect (MSFS 2020 and 2024): flight phases, altitude profile, ETA, top of descent
- Every landing analysed: rate, G-force, bounces, touchdown zone, and a grade from F to A+
- Voice copilot that calls out your flight, on the audio output you choose

📒 REMEMBER
- Automatic logbook with search and filters, careers with ranks, IVAO tours, hangar, achievements and personal records
- Phone and tablet companion app, even offline

📲 MSFS 2024 EFB APP — included
FlightBrief right on the cockpit tablet of every MSFS 2024 aircraft: Briefing, Logbook, Career and Live. No alt-tab mid-approach.

🎥 MADE FOR STREAMERS
- Ready-made OBS overlays: briefing card, live flight bar, landing card, alerts, passengers manifest
- A Twitch bot your viewers actually use: !eta, !metar, !route… every command renamable
- Landing bets and Twitch predictions on your touchdown rate
- Your chat boards the flight: viewers earn miles, collect passport stamps and get a postcard at every arrival
- Turbulence! Chat types !seatbelt in time or loses miles, and a duty free shop to spend them on badges, titles and name colors
- Viewers show up on your map with !vatsim or !ivao, and your public logbook website publishes itself to GitHub Pages
- Stream Deck plugin to run it all

📦 INSTALL
1. Run FlightBrief-Setup.exe (Windows 10/11). If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: "More info" > "Run anyway".
2. Optional EFB app: in FlightBrief, Profile > MSFS 2024 EFB > Install, or copy the "llcenlive-efb-flightbrief" folder into your Community folder.
FlightBrief updates itself after that.

English, French and Spanish. Open source on GitHub (LLCenLive/FlightBrief).
