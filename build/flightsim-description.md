FlightBrief — flight briefing, logbook, career and live tracking for MSFS, made for content creators

FlightBrief is a free Windows companion app for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024. It prepares your flights, tracks them live through SimConnect, keeps your logbook and careers, and gives streamers ready-to-use OBS overlays and a Twitch bot.

FLIGHT
- Briefing: SimBrief import (OFP, route, TOC/TOD), weather, live map with online VATSIM / IVAO controllers and your friends flying online
- Live tracking: phases, altitude profile, landing analysis (rate, G, bounces, touchdown zone) with a landing grade
- Logbook with search and filters, careers with ranks, IVAO tours, hangar, achievements, personal records
- Voice copilot and phone / tablet companion app

MSFS 2024 EFB APP
- FlightBrief on the cockpit tablet of every MSFS 2024 aircraft: Briefing, Logbook, Career and Live
- Included in this download (folder "MSFS 2024 EFB (Community)"), or installed in one click from FlightBrief

FOR STREAMERS
- OBS overlays: briefing card, live flight bar, alerts (landing card, bets, achievements), passengers manifest
- Twitch bot: flight commands (!eta, !metar, !route…), all renamable with their own cooldown
- Landing bets and Twitch predictions, chat passengers earning miles, passport stamps, postcards
- Turbulence alerts (!seatbelt or lose miles), duty free shop to spend miles on badges, titles and name colors
- Viewers can show up on your map with !vatsim / !ivao, public logbook website published to GitHub Pages
- Stream Deck plugin

INSTALLATION
1. Run FlightBrief-Setup.exe (Windows 10/11). If SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, click "More info" then "Run anyway".
2. Optional: copy the "llcenlive-efb-flightbrief" folder into your MSFS 2024 Community folder, or use Profile > MSFS 2024 EFB > Install the EFB app in FlightBrief.
FlightBrief updates itself after installation.

Languages: English, French, Spanish. Everything runs locally on your PC; no account needed.
Source code and release notes on GitHub: LLCenLive/FlightBrief.
