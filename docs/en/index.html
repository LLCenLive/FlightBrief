How it works

One flight, three moments.

FlightBrief is with you before, during and after every flight — without juggling ten windows.

1

You prepare

Your flight plan imports from SimBrief in one click, and your briefing card appears on your stream right away.

  • “Where to fly today?”: weather, online ATC and events
  • SimBrief import and airport charts in one click
  • METAR / TAF weather and calculators
2

You're flying

Start tracking: altitude, speed, flight phases and progress show live — on your PC and on your tablet.

  • Voice copilot: callouts, reminders and cabin announcements
  • Tablet EFB with online ATC
  • Live top of descent and arrival time
3

You keep everything

On arrival, the flight joins your logbook with its track, your touchdown rate and its statistics.

  • Landing graded from A+ to F, approach replayed in 3D
  • Scratch map, airport challenges and Flight Wrapped
  • Careers, tours and hangar with stats for every aircraft
New · in flight

A real copilot in your cockpit.

FlightBrief reads your aircraft's state in the simulator: lights, gear, flaps, parking brake, altimeter setting. A voice calls out every stage of the flight, watches your approach and reminds you of what you forget — like a real copilot.

  • Takeoff, climb and cruise: 80 knots, positive rate, standard altimeter, FL100, level reached, top of descent
  • Approach: 1000 ft stable or not, 500, minimums, then 100, 50, 40, 30, 20 (“Retard” on Airbus), 10
  • GPWS-style alerts: sink rate, bank angle, gear not down, 250 kt below FL100, low fuel
  • Cabin announcements, touchdown rate and status report on demand — everything set category by category
  • Your tablet becomes an EFB: map with VATSIM and IVAO ATC, flight plan and airport charts
  • Stream Deck plugin: tracker, screenshot, copilot, flight info on the keys, Stream Deck + dials

For streamers · your Twitch chat

Your chat comes aboard.

FlightBrief's bot answers your viewers' questions about the current flight, and starts a bet on your landing at top of descent. Whoever guesses the touchdown rate most accurately wins, and their name appears on screen.

  • !eta, !route, !metar, !aircraft, !alt, !landing : the answers come straight from your flight
  • Paris !bet 180 opened and closed automatically, on-screen podium and top bettors leaderboard
  • Give it any name you like. Without a connected account, it still reads the chat and replies on the overlay
  • On board: viewers board, earn miles, stamp their passport and get flown over
  • Twitch predictions: chat wagers channel points on your touchdown rate
  • Not streaming? One switch and all the live features disappear

New · every landing

Your touchdown, dissected.

FlightBrief reads the exact vertical speed at the moment of impact, measured by the simulator — not an approximation. It grades your landing and lets you relive it in 3D.

  • Grade from A+ to F: rate, G-force, bounces, distance from threshold, centreline offset
  • Wind at touchdown and crosswind component, calculated on the real runway
  • Final approach replayed in 3D, compared with the 3° glide path
  • 26 achievements to unlock: butter, night flight, globetrotter, round the world…

FAQ

Everything you're wondering.

I don't stream: is FlightBrief for me?

Yes. Voice copilot, tablet EFB, flight ideas, logbook, career, scratch map and Flight Wrapped have nothing to do with streaming. On first launch, leave “I stream live” off: the Live tool, Twitch and On board tabs disappear (you can turn them back on in the Profile).

Does the voice copilot work with every aircraft?

It reads the simulator's standard variables (altitude, speed, gear, flaps, lights, altimeter…), which most aircraft keep up to date. With some very advanced add-ons (Fenix, PMDG…), a reminder that depends on their own systems simply stays silent; altitude, speed and height callouts work with all of them.

Can I bring my history from another tool?

Yes: Logbook → “Import a flight history”. Export your flights as CSV from Little Navmap, Volanta or a spreadsheet; FlightBrief recognises the columns by itself and creates no duplicates.

Does it work with my Stream Deck?

Yes: classic Stream Deck, XL, Mini and Stream Deck + (dials and touch strip). Profile → Stream Deck → “Install the plugin”: it installs in one click, already paired with your PC.

Is it really free?

Yes, and it will stay that way: no paid version, no subscription, no ads. FlightBrief is a personal project shared with the community, under the MIT open-source licence.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere: everything is saved in a file on your PC, with an automatic daily backup. There's no account to create and nothing is sent over the Internet. Even the mobile app syncs directly with your PC over your Wi-Fi.

Does the Twitch bot need my password?

No. For it to read your chat, just enter your channel name. For it to reply, you register your own Twitch application once (a wizard guides you, 2 minutes), then connect an account (yours or one created for the bot) on Twitch's official page, with a simple code. FlightBrief never sees your password, and access is stored encrypted on your PC.

How do landing bets work?

At top of descent, the bot announces the bets. Your viewers type !bet followed by a rate in ft/min (for example !bet 180). Bets close on short final, and at touchdown the closest guess to the real rate wins: their name shows on screen and they climb the leaderboard. Everything is set in the Twitch tab.

Windows shows a warning — is that normal?

Yes. The installer isn't signed (a signing certificate costs several hundred euros a year), so Windows SmartScreen asks for confirmation the first time. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The code is public on GitHub if you want to check what it does.

Which simulators are supported?

Live tracking works with Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 and 2024) via SimConnect. The briefing, logbook, career, VATSIM / IVAO ATC and overlays work without a simulator. X-Plane support is being looked into.

How do I install the app on my phone?

In FlightBrief, Admin tab → Mobile app: enable access and scan the QR code. A page guides you step by step (only once), then you add the app to your home screen. It works on iPhone and Android.

Does the mobile app work without my PC?

Yes, for viewing: your logbook, careers, tours and hangar stay available anywhere, even in airplane mode. To update them, or to follow a flight live, your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as your PC, with FlightBrief open.

What about Mac or Linux?

FlightBrief is distributed for Windows, because Microsoft Flight Simulator runs on Windows. On Mac or Linux, you can run it from the source code (without live flight tracking).
