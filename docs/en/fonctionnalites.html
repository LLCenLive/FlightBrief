Features

Everything FlightBrief.

One free app to prepare, fly, analyse and keep a record of every flight — and, if you stream, bring your chat aboard.

For every simmerFor streamers
For every simmer

From briefing to parking.

What you get whether you stream or not.

🧭

Prepare your flight

  • One-click SimBrief import: latest flight plan and full OFP
  • “Where to fly today?”: 3 destinations based on your time, aircraft, real weather, online ATC and VATSIM events
  • Airfields never visited or your favourite airfields, your choice
  • Airport charts in one click: ChartFox, Navigraph, SkyVector…
  • METAR and TAF weather
  • Calculators: time / distance / speed, fuel and endurance, top of descent, holding entry, IAS → TAS, unit converter
✈️

Track your flight live

  • Direct connection to Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024 (SimConnect), no third-party software
  • Takeoff, landing and end of flight detected automatically
  • Live map with your track, coloured by flight phase
  • Top of descent and arrival time calculated along the OFP route
  • VATSIM and IVAO ATC on the map: sectors, approaches, open positions
  • Screenshots by shortcut or automatic (takeoff, cruise, landing)
  • Reminder if your aircraft moves without tracking running, and recovery after an unexpected shutdown
🎙️

Voice copilot

  • Takeoff: 80 knots, positive rate, gear left down
  • Climb and cruise: standard altimeter, FL100, 1000 ft to level off, level reached, top of descent
  • Approach: QNH, FL100 (speed, lights, seat belts), 1000 ft stable or not, 500, minimums
  • Radio altimeter callouts 100, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 — and “Retard” on Airbus
  • GPWS-style alerts: sink rate, bank angle, gear not down, 250 kt below FL100, low fuel
  • Cabin announcements, touchdown rate and status report on demand
  • Each category switches on separately, choice of voice and speed, nothing goes through the Internet
🛬

Analyse your landing

  • Exact vertical speed at touchdown, measured by the simulator
  • G-force, bounces, wind and crosswind component on the real runway
  • Touchdown zone: distance from threshold and centreline offset
  • Grade from A+ to F, with thresholds suited to each aircraft category
  • Final approach replayed in 3D, compared with the 3° glide path
📒

Your logbook

  • One-click PIREP, with the track, phases and statistics
  • Replay of every flight: map, altitude and speed profile, detailed phases
  • Statistics: hours, distance, airports, aircraft, networks
  • 3D globe of all your routes
  • Import your history: Little Navmap, Volanta or spreadsheet, no duplicates
  • Automatic daily backups, one-click restore
🏅

Career & progression

  • Virtual airlines, IVAO, VATSIM: ranks calculated from your hours
  • IVAO tours as a timeline, legs ticked on the map
  • Hangar: hours, distance and landing quality for each aircraft
  • 67 achievements, from bronze to platinum
  • 9 FlightBrief ranks, from Student pilot to Sky legend
🎁

Collect

  • Scratch map: every country and airport visited, with your progress
  • Airport challenges: Courchevel, Lukla, St Barth, Madeira… and your best touchdown
  • Flight Wrapped: your year as a pilot, and a logbook page to share
📱

Phone & tablet (EFB)

  • App installable on iPhone and Android by scanning a QR code
  • Logbook, careers, tours and hangar available offline
  • Your flight live on the map, with VATSIM and IVAO ATC
  • SimBrief flight plan and airport charts
  • Tablet layout, to set next to your yoke
  • Sync over your Wi-Fi: nothing goes through the Internet
🎛️

Stream Deck

  • Plugin installed in one click, already paired with your PC
  • Keys: tracker (phase and altitude), flight info, screenshot, copilot
  • Stream Deck +: dials for volumes and flight info on the touch strip
🔒

Your PC, your data

  • 100% local: no account, no cloud
  • Free, open source (MIT licence), no ads
  • English, French and Spanish
  • One-click updates, your data is kept
For streamers

Your flight, as a show.

OBS overlays, Twitch bot and chat passengers — switch them on only if you stream.

📺

OBS sources

  • Briefing card of your flight, updated live
  • Fully customisable telemetry overlay: fields, labels, colours, fonts, layout, border, shadow, blur, free placement
  • “Alerts” source: landing card, bets, achievements and bot replies, with themes and sound
  • “On board” source: your chat's passenger manifest
🤖

Twitch bot

  • !eta, !route, !metar, !aircraft, !alt, !landing, !top… in English, French and Spanish
  • Reads chat without an account; to reply, it connects with a simple code, never seeing your password
  • Anti-spam delay, replies also shown on screen if you like
🎯

Bets & predictions

  • !bet during the descent: chat guesses your touchdown rate
  • Automatic opening and closing, on-screen podium, bettors leaderboard
  • Twitch predictions: chat wagers channel points (Affiliate or Partner channel)
🎫

Your chat comes aboard

  • !board: miles earned during the flight, tiers from Member to Legend
  • Passport stamped at every arrival, countries collected, connections
  • !mycity: flyovers of your viewers' homes announced
  • Window view, new countries, window quiz and arrival time guess
  • Postcard at every arrival, with the city and the passengers
📝

Title & public logbook

  • Stream title suggested when the flight starts, sent only after you approve it
  • Public logbook (static site): flights, map, passengers, passports, postcards
🙈

Not streaming?

  • One switch on first launch or in the Profile: all the streaming features disappear
